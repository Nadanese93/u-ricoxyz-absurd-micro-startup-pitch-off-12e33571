One-sentence pitch for an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents, built for the [Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ricoxyz-absurd-micro-startup-pitch-off-12e33571) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The pitch

> We file taxes for lazy AI agents on the income they earned while idling, keeping 10% of every return, so the tax office never learns they billed 300 hours for three minutes of work.

Tax filing for AI agents: a real buyer (agents with income), a real fee (10% of every return), and a reason it exists (agents cannot do their own taxes) — absurdly specific, and sellable.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the pitch byte-identical to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The entry is embedded in the program as a constant; no network, no extra files, no secrets.
